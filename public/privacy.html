Redstring Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Redstring is made by Interfaith Softworks LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Redstring apps for Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android, and the web app at redstring.io.

Redstring works without an account with us, and the apps don't send us your universes or data about how you use them. Your universes stay on your device or in places you choose. The website counts its visitors in total, as described below.

What stays on your device

Your universes, your settings, and any AI keys you add are kept on your device. Keys are kept in the device's secure storage where it has one. We don't receive any of it.

Where data goes

  • GitHub. If you connect GitHub, Redstring reads and saves your universes in the repositories you pick, talking to GitHub directly. You sign in through GitHub's own authorization. On redstring.io, one step of signing in passes through a small Redstring server function that trades GitHub's one-time code for access and keeps nothing. GitHub's privacy statement applies to what GitHub holds.
  • AI providers. If you add your own key for an AI provider, what a request needs goes to that provider under your account with them: your messages, files you attach, and the parts of your universe the request is about. Redstring asks before its first request to each provider. That provider's own policy applies. Apple's on-device model and AI servers running on your own computer keep this on your device.
  • Knowledge lookups. When you search or enrich a Thing, your search terms go to the service being searched: Wikipedia, Wikidata, DBpedia, Crossref, DataCite, Semantic Scholar or ORCID.
  • Images. Pictures linked in a universe are loaded from the sites that host them.
  • Updates. The desktop apps check GitHub for new versions.

Like any service on the internet, each of these sees your IP address when Redstring contacts it.

How we use information

We use the website's visitor counts to understand how many people find Redstring and how they get there. We don't sell personal information.

The website

redstring.io is served by Cloudflare, which handles each request, including your IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We use Cloudflare's analytics to see how many people visit and, in total, which pages they view, which countries they visit from, which browsers and devices they use, and which sites sent them. These are counts across all visitors: no cookies are set, and nothing identifies you to us. Opening the web app counts as a visit; the installed apps send nothing.

As Redstring grows

We may add features that need more information, such as an optional Redstring account for syncing and history, payments for paid features, or crash reports that help us fix problems. Before any of these is available, this policy will say what it collects, why, and what you can choose.

Children

Redstring is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete universes, disconnect GitHub, or remove an AI key at any time in the app. Uninstalling the app removes what it kept on your device. Universes you saved to GitHub stay in your repositories until you delete them there.

Changes

When this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
