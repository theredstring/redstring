## What changed
- **`src/components/canvas/layers/HurtleOrb.jsx`** (new). It's the first file in the `layers/` folder from the MAP's target structure.
  - It owns the flight's rAF loop.
  - It writes the orb's left, top, width, height, z-index and opacity straight to its `<div data-hurtle-orb>`.
  - It moved over with its detent track (`HURTLE_DETENT_STEP`).
  - `hurtleFrame(flight, progress)` is the flight's shape as a pure function: the easing, the balloon to 1.9×, the z-index steps and the fade.
- **NodeCanvas:**
  - `hurtleFlight` is set once at launch.
  - `handleHurtleLand` clears it after the haptic and `openGraphTabAndBringToTop`, as before.
  - Gone: `runHurtleAnimation`, the per-frame `setHurtleAnimation`, `hurtleAnimationRef` and its unmount cleanup, and the inline orb JSX.
- **B-04 (separate commit):**
  - `startHurtleAnimationFromPanel` now reads `zoomLevelRef` instead of parsing the `<svg>`'s `style.transform`, which stopped carrying the camera long ago. The panel orb was always 30 px; it now scales with zoom like the node launch.
  - `startHurtleAnimation` lists `canvasSize` as a dependency.
- **One visible difference:** the orb now starts as the 1 px dot the old loop drew from its first frame on. Before, the very first paint, before any frame ran, showed it at full size for one frame.
