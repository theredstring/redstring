# P2.03c report: edge selection moves to `canvasUIStore` (D-22)

**Status:** done (orchestrator).

## Decision (D-22)
Move edge selection out of graphStore, as the P2.01 kickoff research (§3) recommended.
- **Cost of staying:** every edge-selection write went through graphStore's middleware: `countUserData` twice (~2 × 2,100 keys on Chambers), a patch listener, and a SaveCoordinator notification that re-armed the 500 ms `sendToWorker` debounce, a structured clone of every persisted key plus a hash, for a change that can't move the hash.
- **Nothing depends on it being in graphStore.** The bridge doesn't serialize it, it isn't persisted, and history filters its patches.

## What changed
- **canvasUIStore:** `selectedEdgeId`, and `selectedEdgeIds` with Set equality and a copy of the caller's iterable, plus `setSelectedEdgeId` / `setSelectedEdgeIds` / `addSelectedEdgeId` / `removeSelectedEdgeId` / `clearSelectedEdgeIds`. Each is a no-op when nothing changes.
- **graphStore:** the two fields are gone from its state (so a missed reader fails loudly). The five action names remain as shims that forward to canvasUIStore without calling graphStore's `set`, so NodeCanvas's ~32 `storeActions.*` calls, the hooks, SelfLoopEdge and TypeList are unchanged.
- **`removeEdge`** now prunes both selections in canvasUIStore. It used to clear only `selectedEdgeId`, leaving a deleted edge in the multi-selection.
- **Readers:** NodeCanvas subscribes to canvasUIStore, and `useNodeDrag` (drag-time edge geometry) and `useGamepad` (mode sync) read it with `getState()`.
- **Tests:** the harness, contract, smoke and render-budget seeds; the e2e `selectedEdgeIds` helper; `setSelectedEdgeIds.test.js`, rewritten to check the shims, the P1.09 no-op guard, that graphStore isn't notified, and the `removeEdge` pruning.
- NodeCanvas.jsx: 16,320 (unchanged).
