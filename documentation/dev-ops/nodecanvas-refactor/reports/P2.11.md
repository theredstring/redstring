# P2.11 report: `CanvasShell` owns the layout

**Status:** done (orchestrator). **Commits:** `f1ceafa` (screenshot tool), `e90dc32` (P2.11).

## What changed
- **`src/components/canvas/hosts/CanvasShell.jsx`** (memo, no props), rendered by App. It holds:
  - the `.node-canvas-container` (styles and `tabIndex` moved as they were)
  - `HeaderHost`
  - the row: left `PanelHost`, `<Profiler id="NodeCanvas"><NodeCanvas/></Profiler>`, right `PanelHost`
  - `TypeListHost` (dropped in the landscape shell, as before)
  - an overlay slot: a `display: contents` div after TypeList
- **NodeCanvas** returns only the `.canvas-area` div and `HurtleOrb`, plus its screen-level overlays. Those are SaveStatusDisplay, the control panels, the carousel, the colour pickers, the modals and the debug overlay. They move to hosts in P2.06 and P5.
  - The overlays are portalled into the shell's slot through `CanvasOverlaySlot` context (`hosts/canvasOverlaySlot.js`). Because the slot has no box and sits where they used to render, their layout and painting order are unchanged.
  - Rendered without a shell (the jsdom tests), NodeCanvas keeps them inline. While the slot isn't attached yet (one layout-phase commit at mount), it renders nothing, so nothing mounts twice.
- **App** no longer imports NodeCanvas. The Profiler test now checks the shell: one mount, one nested update when the slot attaches, and an App re-render (dark mode) doesn't reach NodeCanvas.
- NodeCanvas.jsx: 17,962 → **17,945**; budget lowered.

## Deviation from the card
- The card's "NodeCanvas returns only the canvas-area subtree" holds except for the overlays. They still read NodeCanvas state, so they stay until their hosts exist; the portal keeps their DOM position.
