## New findings

- **NEW-1 (timing, benign).** `paramsRef.current` is assigned during render.
  - So the handler now sees the props of the most recent **render**. Before, it saw the props of the last render whose passive effect had re-registered it.
  - The two differ only in a small window: after a render, before its effects flush, or during a concurrent render that React later discards.
  - The movement loop already accepts this. If exact committed-props semantics were ever wanted, assign `paramsRef.current` in a `useLayoutEffect`. That's a hook-local change, and I didn't make it.

- **NEW-2 (listener order, benign).** Re-adding the listener on every render kept moving it to the **end** of window's keydown bubble listeners. It now stays where it was first registered.
  - I checked `src`: no keydown handler reads `e.defaultPrevented`.
  - The only `stopImmediatePropagation`, in `UnifiedSelector.jsx:175`, is a **capture** listener, so it runs first either way.
  - Side effect in our favour: before, a discrete re-render between two listeners of the same dispatch could remove the old listener before its turn, and the listener added in its place would not fire for that event, so a shortcut could be silently dropped. That can no longer happen.

- **NEW-3 (dead params, Lane A cleanup).** The hook still receives `panOffset` and `zoomLevel`, the settled values, from NodeCanvas (~13555). Nothing reads them any more: the handler never did, and the loop uses the refs.
  - They only end up in `props`/`paramsRef`.
  - Dropping them from the NodeCanvas call site and the hook signature is a P1.02-style dead-parameter cleanup. It needs a NodeCanvas edit, so I didn't do it.
